- One press of Up brings you back from a title's episodes or cast to the
  buttons at the top of its page. It used to take two.
- A long episode title or cast name scrolls while it is highlighted, rather
  than stopping at three dots. The rows on Home have always done this; the
  episodes and the cast on a title's page now do it too.
- Removing something from Continue watching leaves the highlight where it
  was, and the next title moves into the space. It used to jump back to the
  first title on the row.

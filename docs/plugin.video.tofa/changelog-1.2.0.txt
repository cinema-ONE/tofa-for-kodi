- Needs tofa media server 0.12.0 or newer. An older server still works,
  but you are told once per session that some screens may be incomplete.
- Mark a title Seen it or Not interested from its options menu: on
  Discover, on Suggested for You, and on a title you don't have.
- Browse opens on Movies, TV Shows and Other, as tofa groups your
  libraries, instead of one tile per library.
- A person's page lists their whole filmography at once, including the
  films in your library that they directed or wrote.
- Show pages follow your profile's Missing episodes setting in tofa:
  Automatic, Always show or Never show.
- Turning subtitles on skips commentary and short forced tracks when a
  full one is there, and the track list labels forced and commentary
  tracks.

- Opening Options on a title's page, and starting playback, is quick again
  whenever the file plays directly.
- When a title cannot start because the server is busy or cannot be reached,
  the message now offers Try again, and when the server cannot convert it
  fast enough, Lower quality.
- Choosing a different subtitle while your server converts a video now shows
  the one you picked, instead of switching back to one chosen earlier.
- A film that belongs to a series, the James Bond films for instance, now
  lists the rest of that series first under More Like This, including the
  ones not in your library yet.
- The Who's watching? screen is closer to the other tofa apps: a larger lock
  on protected profiles, a gentle lift and glow on the one you are about to
  choose, a ring on the profile you are using, and a background tinted in
  the chosen profile's colour.
- A season with only some of its episodes in your library now counts what
  you have watched against the episodes you can play.
- The language lists in Settings now offer the same 42 languages as the
  other tofa apps, and each Settings page remembers where you were on it.
- Skipping forward or back shows the time you will land on, and the bubble
  leaves as soon as the jump is made.
- Smaller touches to match the other tofa apps: quieter format labels on a
  title's page, the version at the foot of Settings, your server's name
  without a library count, and Sign Out's confirmation starting on Cancel.

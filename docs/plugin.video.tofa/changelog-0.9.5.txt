- The title page no longer keeps you waiting. The artwork and logo used to
  appear at once and the Play row a moment later, which on a television is the
  gap where you are already reaching for the remote. For a series it asked the
  server for the same watch progress three times over and built the whole
  episode grid before drawing the buttons; it now asks once and draws the
  buttons first.
- Skip Intro and Skip Credits no longer trap the remote. While one of those
  buttons was showing you could not reach the progress bar or the transport
  controls at all, and only Back or a press of the button itself got you out.
  Up now goes to the progress bar and down to play/pause.
- Up Next opens when the credits actually start, instead of thirty seconds
  before the file ends. On a film or an episode whose credits tofa has
  detected, that is the moment the rail is for. Skip Credits no longer appears
  alongside it offering the same thing a few seconds earlier; it stays for the
  last episode of a series, where nothing else offers to move on.
- Pausing now holds the Up Next countdown. It used to keep running while the
  film was stopped, so a pause with the rail up could return to find the next
  episode already playing. It carries on from where it was when you resume.
- The pause screen's synopsis keeps the bottom of its letters, and scrolls
  when it is too long to fit rather than stopping mid-sentence.
- The title page's synopsis no longer shows the top of a fifth line it has no
  room for.
- This version needs tofa server 0.9.31 or newer. 0.9.30 was withdrawn by
  tofa; its Windows update could leave a server refusing to start, and on
  Linux it would not install at all.

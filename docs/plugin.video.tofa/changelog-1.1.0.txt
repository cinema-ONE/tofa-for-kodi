- Household viewing: people in your household who have their own tofa
  account can now watch on this TV as themselves, with their own history,
  watchlist and Continue Watching. Turn it on under Settings > Account, on
  the account owner's own profile; Who's watching then lists them after your
  profiles. tofa asks who's watching each time it starts, and again after 15
  minutes away.
- When a household member's session ends, tofa goes back to Who's watching
  from whichever screen you are on.
- Settings > Account is laid out as on the Apple TV app: This TV, then
  Account. Manage account shows the code to scan.
- Collections can be sorted by name or by size. Sorted by name, an A-Z rail
  beside the film series shows just the ones under a letter.
- Inside a collection, Unwatched and Filter now work: by watch status,
  format and year, wherever the collection's titles carry them.
- History lists up to 100 titles, so a long run of one show's episodes no
  longer crowds everything else out. A title that has left your library
  shows once.
- The A-Z rail is now the size of the Apple TV app's.
- When the server cannot be reached at start, tofa now says so instead of
  showing empty screens.
- Fixed a TV losing its tofa cloud sign-in, which made the Account page show
  a username instead of your email address. A TV that already lost it needs
  signing out and in again once.
- Fixed Settings cutting off the last of four choices under a preview.
- Fixed Collections drawing the film series heading over your second row of
  collections.

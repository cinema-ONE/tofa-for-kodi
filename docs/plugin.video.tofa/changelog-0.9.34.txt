- Styled subtitles keep their look when your server converts the video:
  signs and captions stay where they were placed, in their own fonts, sizes
  and colours, instead of becoming plain text at the bottom of the screen.
  Skipping forward or back in a converted video now reloads them, so they
  stay in step with the picture.
- Subtitles stored as pictures (PGS) now also show when your server converts
  the video, for films where your server has already prepared them. Others
  are left out of the list rather than shown incomplete.
- The first card in each row now shows its full glow when selected.

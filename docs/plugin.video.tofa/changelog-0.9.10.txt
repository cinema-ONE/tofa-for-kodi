- Long titles are no longer cut off on the home screen and the details page.
  Where something has no logo artwork of its own, tofa shows its name as text
  -- and that text was set so large that a long name ran most of the way across
  the screen and was still cut short. It is smaller now and wraps onto a second
  line, so you get the whole name.
- tofa asks once more to add its fonts to your skin, because the change above
  needed a text size it had not installed before. It is the same one-off
  question as always and Kodi restarts straight after; nothing else changes.
- Subtitles that sit in separate files beside a film now play properly.
  Where a film's subtitles are in a pair of files next to it rather than
  inside it, tofa used to either re-encode the whole picture to paint
  them on -- costing quality -- or not offer them at all. They are now sent
  across as they are and drawn on top, so the picture is untouched. Needs
  tofa server 0.9.32.
- Video your server has to convert on the way to you can now arrive as HEVC
  instead of H.264, which holds more detail at the same bandwidth. tofa had
  never said it could take anything but H.264, so it always got the older one.
  This applies only to conversion -- anything that plays untouched is
  unaffected -- and only once whoever runs your server has switched HEVC on
  for it, which is off to begin with.
- This version needs tofa server 0.9.32 or newer. On an older server the
  sidecar subtitles above will not appear and converted video keeps coming as
  H.264; everything else works as before.

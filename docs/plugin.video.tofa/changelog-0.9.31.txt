- The picture in the scrub preview is no longer squashed or wrong on a
  programme that is not widescreen. On a 4:3 or a very wide film the preview
  had been showing the frame at the wrong shape, and drifting further from
  the right moment the nearer the end you scrubbed. It now shows the correct
  frame at the programme's own shape, inside the same preview box.
- The chapter name shown while you scrub is no longer a second copy of the
  time. Some files name their chapters after the moment they begin, so the
  readout under the preview read "17:43  00:02:42.662" -- the position, and
  then the position again in another form. Those chapters now show their
  number instead, as chapters with no name at all always have, and so do
  any named with nothing but a digit or two.
- The year beside a part-watched episode on the home screen is now the
  episode's own, not the year the series began. A 2025 episode of a show
  that started in 2023 used to read 2023, next to that episode's title and
  its synopsis.
- The playback stats panel now names the subtitle format twice: the one in
  your file, and the one the add-on actually fetches. Where a styled
  subtitle is being taken as plain text -- which the add-on does on purpose,
  so subtitles look like the rest of the app -- the second one is
  highlighted. Nothing about the subtitles themselves has changed.
- This release needs tofa server 0.10.0 or newer. On an older server the
  add-on still runs, and says so once.

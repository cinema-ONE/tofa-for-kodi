- Home can add a Ready to binge row: whole seasons you can start from the
  beginning. Its cards open on that season.
- Request buttons now follow your server, so they also work without Radarr
  or Sonarr, and go when your requests are switched off.
- Browse follows your server's layout, by type or by library, and a library
  of films and shows together opens on all its titles.
- If your server has Discover switched off, its tab goes, and Search shows
  only what is on your server.
- Needs tofa media server 0.13.0 or newer. An older server still works,
  but you are told once per session that some screens may be incomplete.

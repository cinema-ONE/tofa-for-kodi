- Play Next now always starts the next episode at the beginning. It could
  occasionally begin a minute or two in instead, which happened because the
  add-on handed Kodi the next episode without stopping the previous one
  first. That shortcut existed to avoid a brief display-mode change between
  episodes; if you would rather not see that change, Kodi's own setting
  under Settings > Player > Videos > "Adjust display refresh rate" has an
  "On start" option that leaves the display alone when playback stops.

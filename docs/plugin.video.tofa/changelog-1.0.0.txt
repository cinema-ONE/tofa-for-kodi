- A new top bar, matching tofa's Apple TV app 2.0: the fox on its own,
  Home, Browse, Discover and Search as plain tabs, and Settings as a gear
  beside your profile picture. Select your profile picture to switch
  profiles.
- Discover matches the Apple TV app too: Now, Acclaimed, Genres and Decades
  are plain tabs that switch as you move across them, the page uses the full
  width, and the row you are on stays in the same place as you scroll.
- New Filters on Discover: hide watched movies, cinema-only movies or
  upcoming titles, or show only what is in your library.
- The title page's second half is now one page you scroll through, as on
  the Apple TV app: the episode you are on with its synopsis, the seasons
  and episodes, then Cast & Crew, More Like This, More to Discover and an
  About card. The episode you are about to resume is no longer hidden as a
  spoiler, and Specials come after the last season. Its rows use smaller
  posters, as the Apple TV app's do.
- Rows on Home and Discover are spaced like the Apple TV app's.
- The player matches the Apple TV app 2.0: an episode's controls start with
  the episode list, the Playback button (a gauge) adds a Speed setting where
  Kodi allows it, and the stats button simply shows or hides the stats.
- Next Up comes in four styles -- Compact, Minimal, Lower third and Full --
  chosen for each TV under Settings > Playback & Video.
- The stats name how a title is playing in words: Direct Play, Direct Stream
  or Transcode.
- The pause screen and the episode list match the Apple TV app: pausing
  shows the year, episode and its title on their own lines, and the Episodes
  button opens a row of episodes along the bottom of the screen.
- A title watched for less than a minute offers Play again, not Resume.
- Settings matches the Apple TV app 2.0: its pages are tabs along the top,
  with a new Home tab, and This Device's settings now sit under Account and
  Privacy & About. The left side describes the setting you are on, every
  setting fits on one line, and its choices open in a list beside it --
  Fox accent opens a grid of foxes.
- On the Home tab, select a row to move it, hide it or remove one you
  added, and add a Discover or genre row from the two rows below.
- Settings shows a preview for the playback rows, Fox accent and the Home
  tab, drawn over artwork from your own library.
- Browse matches the Apple TV app 2.0: it opens on tiles for your libraries,
  Watchlist, History, Collections and Surprise me, and each opens full
  screen with seven posters across, the genres as a row of buttons, and an
  Unwatched switch beside Sort and Filter.
- Collections shows your own collections first, then film series and sets,
  as wide cards three to a row; yours show four of their posters.
- Sort opens as a panel over the view, with the current sort ticked and its
  direction shown; choose it again to reverse it.
- Behind Browse's tiles, a library shows a slowly drifting wall of its own
  posters, Watchlist and History a row of theirs, Collections one
  collection with the films of it you have, and Surprise me a blurred wall.
- A person's page matches the Apple TV app 2.0: their photo, the part they
  played, how many credits they have and over which years, and a
  Filmography button listing every credit by year. All of their credits now
  appear, where only the first 30 used to.
- Show posters say how many episodes you have left, and finished titles
  carry a tick; Watched marks in Settings turns the ticks off. A title
  without a tofa score shows its TMDB rating instead.
- Hidden episodes follow the Apple TV app: past the first one you have not
  seen, each you have neither started nor finished keeps its details hidden.
- History shows each film or show once, with its year and rating.
- Who's watching matches the Apple TV app 2.0: profiles three to a row on a
  plain background, in your fox's colour, with the app's PIN pad.
- New in Settings: Pause screen (turn it off to keep the controls up while
  paused) and Reduce motion (no zooms, slides or moving backgrounds).
- Down from the top bar now works straight away when you move on from
  Home before it has finished loading.
- After updating, tofa asks once to add a new font to your skin.

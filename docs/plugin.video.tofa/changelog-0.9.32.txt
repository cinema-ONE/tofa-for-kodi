- Holding or tapping left and right during playback now jumps once, when
  you stop pressing, instead of jumping on every press. The counter on
  screen counts the whole movement rather than the last step.
- When a server refuses to start something, it now says why in its own
  words -- that it is busy converting other videos, for instance -- instead
  of the add-on's single "couldn't start this file".
- The list of seasons now shows where you are at a glance: a tick for a
  season you have finished, how many episodes are left in one you are
  partway through, and a plus for a season with nothing in your library.
  Open that season and it says "Not in library", and so does each of its
  episodes -- instead of hiding them behind "Details hidden".
- The pictures on the "Who's watching?" screen are bigger, and sit the way
  they do in the other tofa apps.
- On an episode with nothing before or after it, the two buttons either side
  of play now skip forward and back ten seconds, instead of being greyed out
  with nowhere to go.

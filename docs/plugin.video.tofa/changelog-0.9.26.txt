- The add-on no longer copies its fonts into the skin you are using. They
  travel in a small companion add-on instead, which Kodi installs alongside
  this one, and any copies an earlier version left behind are cleared away.
  Nothing looks different; there is simply less of us in your skin.
- An error message no longer appears while the add-on updates itself. A new
  version arriving while the old one was still closing down could put a Kodi
  error notice on screen for a moment. Nothing was affected by it.

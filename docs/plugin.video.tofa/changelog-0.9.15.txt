- Browse's Collections list now opens straight away. On a large library it
  had to prepare every single collection before it could show you the
  first one, which meant a wait of several seconds each time you opened
  the section -- and much longer the first time. It now prepares only what
  is on screen, and the rest as you scroll to it.
- If you change Kodi skin, tofa now offers to add its fonts to the new
  skin. Previously, saying no to that once meant you were never asked
  again, even on a different skin where the fonts really were missing, and
  tofa's own screens were left using whatever the skin happened to supply.

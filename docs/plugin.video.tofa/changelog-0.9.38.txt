- Needs tofa media server 0.11.0 or newer. An older server still works,
  but you are told once per session that some screens may be incomplete.
- Search now finds collections of every kind: your own, film series like
  James Bond, and tofa's curated sets. The best match appears as a row of
  the titles you have, just below the top result.
- More Like This no longer waits and asks again when a title has nothing
  similar; the server now says so straight away.
- Browse a library by its folders: a View button at the start of the row
  switches between the library's own view and Folders. Libraries of home
  videos open in Folders. Back goes up a level, and selecting the library
  again returns to its top.
- A film or episode split across several files now plays as one: one
  timeline over all its parts, and no picking between "versions" that are
  really part 1 and part 2.
- Cast and crew photos now load from your own server.
- Dolby Vision Profile 5 videos are converted on devices without Dolby
  Vision, instead of playing in the wrong colours.
- Picture subtitles on a converted stream can be chosen again before the
  server has prepared them; they appear once it has the whole track.

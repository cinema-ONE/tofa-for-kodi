- When a title will not load because your server is busy rather than
  unreachable, tofa now says so. It used to tell you to check your
  connection even when the connection was fine and the server was simply
  taking too long to answer, which sent you looking at the one part that
  was working.
- Cast & Crew fills in one go. The photographs used to arrive a few at a
  time, leaving half the panel empty for several seconds on a 4K box; tofa
  now collects them while you are still on the title's main page. The
  People results in Search are quicker for the same reason.
- Discover and Collections fill the same way. Their artwork comes from
  tofa's cloud rather than from your own server, and Kodi was filing every
  picture away itself before it drew it -- on a cold Discover tab that took
  twenty seconds. tofa now fetches what is on screen up front and the rest
  quietly behind it.
- tofa no longer leaves background work and open connections behind when it
  has finished with a screen. On a box left running all day this showed up
  as memory creeping upwards.
- Continue Watching only says how much is left when there is something
  left. Once you finish an episode the next one takes its place on that
  row, and tofa was labelling it with the whole episode's running time as
  though you were already part-way through -- while drawing no progress bar
  under it, which is what gave it away.

- You can re-arrange your Home screen. Settings > Appearance gives every
  row its own controls: move it up, move it down, switch it off, or take
  it off the list if you put it there yourself. The ten rows every tofa
  account starts with can be switched off but not removed, which is how
  tofa's other apps behave. Adding a row is now one control listing
  everything you can add, grouped into Discover lists, genres and Home
  rows.
- If your Home screen has ten rows, the tenth one is now there. The
  add-on had room for nine, so the last row simply did not appear, and
  could not be reached in Settings either. A tofa account now starts with
  ten rows, so this affected anyone who had not changed theirs.
- Settings that offer a few fixed choices now show all of them at once,
  so you can go straight to the one you want instead of stepping through
  the others. This covers the rating badge, preferred quality, whether to
  play the next episode, and the five skip behaviours.
- An episode's own title now appears on the detail screen, at the start
  of the line carrying the year and the rest of the details.
- The note under the Home screen row list showed a stray line of text
  belonging to Kodi's skin instead of tofa's own wording.

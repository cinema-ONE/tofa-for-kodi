- Discover follows the Apple TV app: For You, Trending, Coming Up, Best
  Of, Universes and Genres. Coming Up is a week planner for TV shows and
  films, one column per day; Universes lists every film and TV universe;
  a genre opens its own page, which you can follow.
- Home has a new row, Continue the saga, under Continue Watching: the film
  and TV universes you are part way through, each with the next title to
  watch. Select one to see the whole universe in story or release order.
  A title's page shows the universe it belongs to, with its place in it,
  and Down on a universe lists every title by chapter.
- A universe's page can request what your library is missing, for the
  whole universe or one chapter, if your server has Radarr or Sonarr.
- A title you don't have shows Seen it and Not interested on its page;
  both are marked with a filled icon once set.
- Card menus list their choices in the Apple TV app's order, and Not
  interested takes the card off the row straight away.
- On a title page, the options menu of More Like This, More to Discover
  and a collection's titles now does what it offers: Watchlist and Mark as
  Watched work there, and Seen it and Not interested appear where they
  apply.
- A film resumes the edition you were watching last, wherever you open
  it from, not the first one listed.
- Browse shows your libraries in a row of their own, above Watchlist,
  History, Collections and Surprise me.
- Back on Browse, Discover, Search or Settings now returns to Home, to the
  card you were on.
- Settings > Appearance has Missing episodes: Automatic, Always show or
  Never show.
- Playback Options lists each quality as 1080p, 720p and so on, with the
  rate the server will actually send.
- After updating, tofa asks once more to install its fonts.

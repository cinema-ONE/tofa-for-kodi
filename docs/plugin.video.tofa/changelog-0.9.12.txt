- Coming back to tofa after a break no longer leaves you on a blank page.
  If the app had been sitting idle for a minute or two, the next thing you
  opened could hang for about fifteen seconds and then show a title with no
  artwork and an empty Play button. The connection to your server had gone
  quiet without tofa noticing. It now opens a fresh one instead of waiting
  on the old.
- A title that cannot load now tells you why. Where the page used to come
  up empty, it says whether your server could not be reached, your profile
  needs unlocking again, or the title is no longer in your library -- and
  offers a Retry button.
- Stray marks at the edge of the screen are gone. If you had made the
  interface smaller (Settings > Interface > Skin > Zoom, below 100%), two
  things appeared outside the picture: a small pale tick to the left of the
  player's progress bar, and a sliver of the next card past the right-hand
  end of the rows on Home and Discover. Both sat just off the edge of the
  screen, where a reduced zoom brought them back into view. Nothing about
  these screens has changed at 100%.

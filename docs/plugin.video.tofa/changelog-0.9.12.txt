- A title that cannot load now tells you why. Where the page used to come up
  empty -- no artwork, no synopsis, and a Play button with nothing on it --
  it now says whether your server could not be reached, your profile needs
  unlocking again, or the title is no longer in your library, and offers a
  Retry button.
- Stray marks at the edge of the screen are gone. If you had made the
  interface smaller (Settings > Interface > Skin > Zoom, below 100%), two
  things appeared outside the picture: a small pale tick to the left of the
  player's progress bar, and a sliver of the next card past the right-hand
  end of the rows on Home and Discover. Both sat just off the edge of the
  screen, where a reduced zoom brought them back into view. Nothing about
  these screens has changed at 100%.

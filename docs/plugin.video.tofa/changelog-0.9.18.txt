- Settings > Appearance > "Featured spotlight" now does something on this
  device. It had no effect here, even though switching it off still changed
  what your other tofa apps showed. Turning it off hides the large banner at
  the top of Home -- its title, logo and description -- and gives the space
  to your rows, so another row fits on screen. The artwork behind it stays,
  and still follows whichever card you are on.
- Whichever option you are on in Settings now has an outline around it, so
  you can see where you are before you press anything. This covers the
  choices under Playback & Video, the rating badge under Appearance, and the
  switches beside your Home screen rows.
- Browse > Collections puts each collection's name on a single line now,
  with the number of titles directly beneath it. Space was kept for a second
  line whether or not a name needed one, which left a gap under every name
  that fitted on one. A name too long to fit scrolls while you are on it.
  The rows also sit closer together, so more collections fit on screen.

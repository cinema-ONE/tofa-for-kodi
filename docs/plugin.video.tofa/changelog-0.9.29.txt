- The Skip Intro and Skip Credits buttons no longer stay on screen for the
  whole of a long intro or credits. They now hide after a few seconds, as in
  the other tofa apps, and come back whenever you bring up the player
  controls while the intro or credits are still playing.
- On a series, the line under the title on the Details page now gives the
  year and length of the episode the Play button is offering, rather than the
  year the series started and its average episode length. The synopsis there
  already described that episode.
- Settings > Appearance > Media cards gains "Hide episode spoilers". Details
  already hid the picture of episodes past the one you are on when your
  account asked for it, but only the other tofa apps could switch it; now
  this one can too, and the change follows your account to all of them.

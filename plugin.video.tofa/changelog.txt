0.9.4
- 3D films get their own button in the player controls. On a 3D file it sits
  beside subtitles and audio, and asks the same question the film asks when it
  starts, so you can change your mind part way through. It replaces the 3D row
  under Adjust, which applied every mode as you stepped past it and made the
  television renegotiate the picture on each press.
- The Adjust panel is easier to read: a value and its arrows no longer sit
  tight under the label above them.
- "Report a problem" under Settings -> Privacy & About no longer runs off the
  end of its card. The part that was cut off said where account and server
  trouble goes instead; the issue page the code opens says it now, where it is
  a link rather than an address to copy off a television.

0.9.3
- Report a problem now reaches the people who can fix it. The QR code under
  Settings -> Privacy & About opens this add-on's own issue tracker instead of
  tofa's support desk, which cannot act on a Kodi bug. Account and server
  trouble still goes to tofa, and the caption says which is which.
- A profile unlocked with a PIN no longer locks itself again while you are
  still watching. The unlock now extends as playback continues, so a long
  film no longer ends with the episode unticked and your place forgotten.
- Profile pictures now load however you are connected. Reached through tofa's
  relay rather than over your own network, every profile fell back to its
  initials; the artwork comes from your own server now, which works either
  way, and a picture changed on the web appears here without an update.
- Needs tofa server 0.9.30 or later. Older servers still work, and still say
  so once per Kodi session, but these two fixes are not there.

0.9.2
- Forcing a quality below Original no longer costs you surround. The server
  was being asked for its default stereo AAC; it now delivers E-AC-3 5.1 where
  your audio output can take it, at the same bitrate. On a stereo output with
  no passthrough nothing changes, which is the right answer there.
- Choosing "Original" is now told to the server as a deliberate choice rather
  than looking like no choice at all.
- The audio track no longer comes out in the wrong language when the app
  cannot reach the server for a moment at the start of playback. Your language
  preferences are remembered on the device and used when that happens.
- An explicit subtitle pick no longer stops your preferred audio language from
  being applied, and the reverse.
- Artwork is cleaned up on a schedule instead of accumulating. Settings ->
  This Device gains a storage limit for it and a way to clear it now.
- Artwork downloads once instead of repeatedly on Browse, Search, Discover,
  filmographies, episode lists and the player's episode drawer.

0.9.1
- Browse no longer jumps back to the first row while you scroll. The grid is
  allocated to the library's full length up front and fills in as you go, so
  the scrollbar and count describe the whole library rather than the first
  page.
- Pressing Back now returns to the item you opened, in every section. Opening
  a title from Home used to leave the add-on altogether when you backed out.
- Down from the top menu enters Browse's grid instead of its left menu.
- Discover: a title the server does not hold now has a real detail page --
  artwork, scores, synopsis, an "In cinemas" badge, and only the actions that
  apply to it.
- Detail shows four lines of synopsis instead of three.
- The PIN can be typed on a keyboard or number pad instead of picking digits
  one at a time.
- Returning to playback from Kodi's own menu comes back to this add-on's
  player rather than Kodi's.
- Fixed a startup fault that could stop playback and delay every launch.

0.9.0
- First versioned release. Every major screen is built: Home, Browse,
  Discover, Search, Detail, the player, and Settings.
- Home, Browse, Discover and Search share one persistent window, so moving
  between them keeps its place instead of rebuilding.
- Detail: hero, episode grid with season sidebar, cast and filmography,
  more-like-this, and per-file Edition, Options and playback actions.
- Player: transport, scrubber with scrub thumbnails, chapter and
  intro/outro skipping, audio and subtitle selection, Next Up, and a stats
  overlay.
- Settings: six pages covering account, playback, audio and subtitles,
  appearance, privacy, and this device. Kodi's own settings dialog is no
  longer used.
- Playback negotiates DirectPlay/DirectFile, so Dolby Vision, TrueHD and
  DTS-HD passthrough and refresh-rate matching reach Kodi's VideoPlayer
  untouched.
- Interface fonts and icons are bundled and installed into the active skin
  at runtime, so the add-on looks the same on any skin.

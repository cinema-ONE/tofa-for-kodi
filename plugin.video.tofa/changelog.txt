0.9.0
- First versioned release. Every major screen is built: Home, Browse,
  Discover, Search, Detail, the player, and Settings.
- Home, Browse, Discover and Search share one persistent window, so moving
  between them keeps its place instead of rebuilding.
- Detail: hero, episode grid with season sidebar, cast and filmography,
  more-like-this, and per-file Edition, Options and playback actions.
- Player: transport, scrubber with scrub thumbnails, chapter and
  intro/outro skipping, audio and subtitle selection, Next Up, and a stats
  overlay.
- Settings: six pages covering account, playback, audio and subtitles,
  appearance, privacy, and this device. Kodi's own settings dialog is no
  longer used.
- Playback negotiates DirectPlay/DirectFile, so Dolby Vision, TrueHD and
  DTS-HD passthrough and refresh-rate matching reach Kodi's VideoPlayer
  untouched.
- Interface fonts and icons are bundled and installed into the active skin
  at runtime, so the add-on looks the same on any skin.

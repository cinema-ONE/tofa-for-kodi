0.9.25
- One press of Up brings you back from a title's episodes or cast to the
  buttons at the top of its page. It used to take two.
- A long episode title or cast name scrolls while it is highlighted, rather
  than stopping at three dots. The rows on Home have always done this; the
  episodes and the cast on a title's page now do it too.
- Removing something from Continue watching leaves the highlight where it
  was, and the next title moves into the space. It used to jump back to the
  first title on the row.

0.9.24
- Less work while a film plays. The player was asking Kodi the same question
  five times a second in a way that turned out to be expensive on a set-top
  box; it now asks a cheaper one. Nothing looks different -- it simply leaves
  more of the box free for the picture.
- The Rewatch button on a title's page shows a replay arrow. It had been
  showing a toggle switch.

0.9.23
- Built against tofa media server 0.9.34. An older server is not refused and
  most of the add-on carries on working, but it now says so once per session,
  the same as it always has when it meets a server older than it was built
  for.
- More titles now name their shape. A film or series whose file is stored
  already cropped to the picture -- with no black bars inside the frame --
  showed no aspect ratio at all on the detail page. It now says 2.39:1 or
  2.00:1 like everything else, under About as well, and on each episode.
- "Plays as" no longer promises HDR10+ on a television that cannot show it.
  On those sets a Dolby Vision film now correctly reads as HDR10.

0.9.22
- The detail screen now says what shape a film is: a "2.39:1" badge beside the
  resolution and sound, and the name of that shape under About. A widescreen
  film stored in a 16:9 file used to be described as 16:9, because that is
  what the file says; this reads the picture inside the frame instead.
- More room under the Critics and Audience scores on the About panel. The
  format badges sat almost against them.

0.9.21
- Internal maintenance only. Nothing about the add-on behaves differently.

0.9.20
- A show with hundreds of episodes offers the right episode to play next
  again. Past about five hundred episodes the detail screen could not work
  out how far you had got, so it fell back to offering the first episode of
  series one however deep into the show you were.
- A PIN-locked profile keeps a record of what you watch through a long
  evening. After a few hours the add-on could quietly stop being able to
  save anything: an episode you had just finished was not marked as
  watched, and Continue watching went on offering it from where you left
  it hours earlier. Your profile stayed unlocked throughout, so nothing on
  screen said it had happened.

0.9.19
- The banner at the top of Home no longer gets covered by the first row.
  Moving down from it in 0.9.18 slid the row up over the banner, leaving its
  title, logo and description tangled together and unreadable. Switching
  "Featured spotlight" off still hands that space to your rows.

0.9.18
- Settings > Appearance > "Featured spotlight" now does something on this
  device. It had no effect here, even though switching it off still changed
  what your other tofa apps showed. Turning it off hides the large banner at
  the top of Home -- its title, logo and description -- and gives the space
  to your rows, so another row fits on screen. The artwork behind it stays,
  and still follows whichever card you are on.
- Whichever option you are on in Settings now has an outline around it, so
  you can see where you are before you press anything. This covers the
  choices under Playback & Video, the rating badge under Appearance, and the
  switches beside your Home screen rows.
- Browse > Collections puts each collection's name on a single line now,
  with the number of titles directly beneath it. Space was kept for a second
  line whether or not a name needed one, which left a gap under every name
  that fitted on one. A name too long to fit scrolls while you are on it.
  The rows also sit closer together, so more collections fit on screen.

0.9.17
- You can re-arrange your Home screen. Settings > Appearance gives every
  row its own controls: move it up, move it down, switch it off, or take
  it off the list if you put it there yourself. The ten rows every tofa
  account starts with can be switched off but not removed, which is how
  tofa's other apps behave. Adding a row is now one control listing
  everything you can add, grouped into Discover lists, genres and Home
  rows.
- If your Home screen has ten rows, the tenth one is now there. The
  add-on had room for nine, so the last row simply did not appear, and
  could not be reached in Settings either. A tofa account now starts with
  ten rows, so this affected anyone who had not changed theirs.
- Settings that offer a few fixed choices now show all of them at once,
  so you can go straight to the one you want instead of stepping through
  the others. This covers the rating badge, preferred quality, whether to
  play the next episode, and the five skip behaviours.
- An episode's own title now appears on the detail screen, at the start
  of the line carrying the year and the rest of the details.
- The note under the Home screen row list showed a stray line of text
  belonging to Kodi's skin instead of tofa's own wording.

0.9.16
- Play Next now always starts the next episode at the beginning. It could
  occasionally begin a minute or two in instead, which happened because the
  add-on handed Kodi the next episode without stopping the previous one
  first. That shortcut existed to avoid a brief display-mode change between
  episodes; if you would rather not see that change, Kodi's own setting
  under Settings > Player > Videos > "Adjust display refresh rate" has an
  "On start" option that leaves the display alone when playback stops.

0.9.15
- Browse's Collections list now opens straight away. On a large library it
  had to prepare every single collection before it could show you the
  first one, which meant a wait of several seconds each time you opened
  the section -- and much longer the first time. It now prepares only what
  is on screen, and the rest as you scroll to it.
- If you change Kodi skin, tofa now offers to add its fonts to the new
  skin. Previously, saying no to that once meant you were never asked
  again, even on a different skin where the fonts really were missing, and
  tofa's own screens were left using whatever the skin happened to supply.

0.9.14
- Picture subtitles stored inside a film's own file (VobSub) now appear in
  the subtitle list when the file plays directly. They used to be offered
  only burnt into the picture, which meant re-encoding the whole video for
  a subtitle track. Needs tofa server 0.9.33, which is now the version this
  add-on is built against.
- Collections people make on your server now show up in Browse, ahead of
  the catalogue's own franchise sets. Open one to see what's in it; making
  and editing them stays in tofa's web app for now. Needs tofa server
  0.9.33.
- "Always show subtitles" no longer gives up when no track matches your
  preferred language: it now tries the language of the audio you are
  hearing, then a track with no language label, before leaving subtitles
  off. The same order tofa's web player uses.
- A converted stream that cannot start straight away -- usually because a
  just-stopped stream is still letting go of the server's graphics card --
  now gets a second chance two seconds later instead of an error message.

0.9.13
- When a title will not load because your server is busy rather than
  unreachable, tofa now says so. It used to tell you to check your
  connection even when the connection was fine and the server was simply
  taking too long to answer, which sent you looking at the one part that
  was working.
- Cast & Crew fills in one go. The photographs used to arrive a few at a
  time, leaving half the panel empty for several seconds on a 4K box; tofa
  now collects them while you are still on the title's main page. The
  People results in Search are quicker for the same reason.
- Discover and Collections fill the same way. Their artwork comes from
  tofa's cloud rather than from your own server, and Kodi was filing every
  picture away itself before it drew it -- on a cold Discover tab that took
  twenty seconds. tofa now fetches what is on screen up front and the rest
  quietly behind it.
- tofa no longer leaves background work and open connections behind when it
  has finished with a screen. On a box left running all day this showed up
  as memory creeping upwards.
- Continue Watching only says how much is left when there is something
  left. Once you finish an episode the next one takes its place on that
  row, and tofa was labelling it with the whole episode's running time as
  though you were already part-way through -- while drawing no progress bar
  under it, which is what gave it away.

0.9.12
- A title that cannot load now tells you why. Where the page used to come up
  empty -- no artwork, no synopsis, and a Play button with nothing on it --
  it now says whether your server could not be reached, your profile needs
  unlocking again, or the title is no longer in your library, and offers a
  Retry button.
- Stray marks at the edge of the screen are gone. If you had made the
  interface smaller (Settings > Interface > Skin > Zoom, below 100%), two
  things appeared outside the picture: a small pale tick to the left of the
  player's progress bar, and a sliver of the next card past the right-hand
  end of the rows on Home and Discover. Both sat just off the edge of the
  screen, where a reduced zoom brought them back into view. Nothing about
  these screens has changed at 100%.

0.9.11
- The next episode keeps your audio language. Starting the next episode from
  the Up Next rail could put it on the soundtrack the file itself lists first,
  rather than the language on your profile -- a series whose files carry
  German before English would come back in German at every episode. tofa now
  checks which track is really playing before deciding there is nothing to
  change, and checks once more a moment later that the language it asked for
  is the one you are hearing.

0.9.10
- Long titles are no longer cut off on the home screen and the details page.
  Where something has no logo artwork of its own, tofa shows its name as text
  -- and that text was set so large that a long name ran most of the way across
  the screen and was still cut short. It is smaller now and wraps onto a second
  line, so you get the whole name.
- tofa asks once more to add its fonts to your skin, because the change above
  needed a text size it had not installed before. It is the same one-off
  question as always and Kodi restarts straight after; nothing else changes.
- Subtitles that sit in separate files beside a film now play properly.
  Where a film's subtitles are in a pair of files next to it rather than
  inside it, tofa used to either re-encode the whole picture to paint
  them on -- costing quality -- or not offer them at all. They are now sent
  across as they are and drawn on top, so the picture is untouched. Needs
  tofa server 0.9.32.
- Video your server has to convert on the way to you can now arrive as HEVC
  instead of H.264, which holds more detail at the same bandwidth. tofa had
  never said it could take anything but H.264, so it always got the older one.
  This applies only to conversion -- anything that plays untouched is
  unaffected -- and only once whoever runs your server has switched HEVC on
  for it, which is off to begin with.
- This version needs tofa server 0.9.32 or newer. On an older server the
  sidecar subtitles above will not appear and converted video keeps coming as
  H.264; everything else works as before.

0.9.9
- After you finish an episode, the details page now describes the NEXT
  episode, not the one you just watched. The Play button already moved on to
  the next episode, but the synopsis and the audio and video badges stayed
  behind on the one that had just ended.
- Settings now tells you how this device is reaching your server: connected
  directly, or through tofa's relay. On the relay everything travels through
  tofa, video included, so it can be slower -- and until now nothing on
  screen said which one you were on.
- The Account page in Settings fills in the moment it opens. It used to leave
  the panel blank for a beat while it waited on one slower lookup before
  showing anything.

0.9.8
- A series now carries on from where you are. If you had finished a later
  episode but skipped one earlier on, the details page went back and offered
  the skipped one instead of the episode you were up to.
- Something you have finished no longer offers both Play and Rewatch. Both
  started it from the beginning, so there was nothing to choose between them.
- Playback statistics now report the soundtrack's own channels. Where sound
  is passed straight through to an amplifier, a 5.1 soundtrack was described
  as 7.1: the panel was reading the connection carrying the sound rather than
  the sound itself.

0.9.7
- More Windows fixes, following 0.9.6. tofa could sign in but then forgot
  everything it was told: the profile you picked was never saved, so it asked
  again on every screen and your library came back empty. It could not hold on
  to your sign-in either, which would eventually have logged the device out on
  its own.
- On Windows, tofa's icons and fonts now install. They were silently refused
  before, so the interface fell back to Kodi's own font, every icon showed as
  an empty box, and tofa kept asking permission for something that had already
  failed. Kodi's own seek bar no longer appears over tofa's player there.
- Installing from the repository no longer fails the first time. Adding tofa's
  address to Kodi taught it that part of the update channel did not exist, so
  the add-on could not be found until Kodi was restarted.

0.9.6
- tofa now works on Windows. It would pair with your account and then stop
  there: nothing after that opened, and nothing on screen said why. Only
  Windows was affected, and only once you had signed in -- which is why
  pairing itself always looked fine.
- When something will not play, tofa now tells you instead of leaving you on
  a spinner that never finishes. The usual reason is that the file has been
  moved or renamed since your server last scanned its library, and it says
  so, because a fresh scan is what brings it back.

0.9.5
- The title page no longer keeps you waiting. The artwork and logo used to
  appear at once and the Play row a moment later, which on a television is the
  gap where you are already reaching for the remote. For a series it asked the
  server for the same watch progress three times over and built the whole
  episode grid before drawing the buttons; it now asks once and draws the
  buttons first.
- Skip Intro and Skip Credits no longer trap the remote. While one of those
  buttons was showing you could not reach the progress bar or the transport
  controls at all, and only Back or a press of the button itself got you out.
  Up now goes to the progress bar and down to play/pause.
- Up Next opens when the credits actually start, instead of thirty seconds
  before the file ends. On a film or an episode whose credits tofa has
  detected, that is the moment the rail is for. Skip Credits no longer appears
  alongside it offering the same thing a few seconds earlier; it stays for the
  last episode of a series, where nothing else offers to move on.
- Pausing now holds the Up Next countdown. It used to keep running while the
  film was stopped, so a pause with the rail up could return to find the next
  episode already playing. It carries on from where it was when you resume.
- The pause screen's synopsis keeps the bottom of its letters, and scrolls
  when it is too long to fit rather than stopping mid-sentence.
- The title page's synopsis no longer shows the top of a fifth line it has no
  room for.
- This version needs tofa server 0.9.31 or newer. 0.9.30 was withdrawn by
  tofa; its Windows update could leave a server refusing to start, and on
  Linux it would not install at all.

0.9.4
- 3D films get their own button in the player controls. On a 3D file it sits
  beside subtitles and audio, and asks the same question the film asks when it
  starts, so you can change your mind part way through. It replaces the 3D row
  under Adjust, which applied every mode as you stepped past it and made the
  television renegotiate the picture on each press.
- The Adjust panel is easier to read: a value and its arrows no longer sit
  tight under the label above them.
- "Report a problem" under Settings -> Privacy & About no longer runs off the
  end of its card. The part that was cut off said where account and server
  trouble goes instead; the issue page the code opens says it now, where it is
  a link rather than an address to copy off a television.

0.9.3
- Report a problem now reaches the people who can fix it. The QR code under
  Settings -> Privacy & About opens this add-on's own issue tracker instead of
  tofa's support desk, which cannot act on a Kodi bug. Account and server
  trouble still goes to tofa, and the caption says which is which.
- A profile unlocked with a PIN no longer locks itself again while you are
  still watching. The unlock now extends as playback continues, so a long
  film no longer ends with the episode unticked and your place forgotten.
- Profile pictures now load however you are connected. Reached through tofa's
  relay rather than over your own network, every profile fell back to its
  initials; the artwork comes from your own server now, which works either
  way, and a picture changed on the web appears here without an update.
- Needs tofa server 0.9.30 or later. Older servers still work, and still say
  so once per Kodi session, but these two fixes are not there.

0.9.2
- Forcing a quality below Original no longer costs you surround. The server
  was being asked for its default stereo AAC; it now delivers E-AC-3 5.1 where
  your audio output can take it, at the same bitrate. On a stereo output with
  no passthrough nothing changes, which is the right answer there.
- Choosing "Original" is now told to the server as a deliberate choice rather
  than looking like no choice at all.
- The audio track no longer comes out in the wrong language when the app
  cannot reach the server for a moment at the start of playback. Your language
  preferences are remembered on the device and used when that happens.
- An explicit subtitle pick no longer stops your preferred audio language from
  being applied, and the reverse.
- Artwork is cleaned up on a schedule instead of accumulating. Settings ->
  This Device gains a storage limit for it and a way to clear it now.
- Artwork downloads once instead of repeatedly on Browse, Search, Discover,
  filmographies, episode lists and the player's episode drawer.

0.9.1
- Browse no longer jumps back to the first row while you scroll. The grid is
  allocated to the library's full length up front and fills in as you go, so
  the scrollbar and count describe the whole library rather than the first
  page.
- Pressing Back now returns to the item you opened, in every section. Opening
  a title from Home used to leave the add-on altogether when you backed out.
- Down from the top menu enters Browse's grid instead of its left menu.
- Discover: a title the server does not hold now has a real detail page --
  artwork, scores, synopsis, an "In cinemas" badge, and only the actions that
  apply to it.
- Detail shows four lines of synopsis instead of three.
- The PIN can be typed on a keyboard or number pad instead of picking digits
  one at a time.
- Returning to playback from Kodi's own menu comes back to this add-on's
  player rather than Kodi's.
- Fixed a startup fault that could stop playback and delay every launch.

0.9.0
- First versioned release. Every major screen is built: Home, Browse,
  Discover, Search, Detail, the player, and Settings.
- Home, Browse, Discover and Search share one persistent window, so moving
  between them keeps its place instead of rebuilding.
- Detail: hero, episode grid with season sidebar, cast and filmography,
  more-like-this, and per-file Edition, Options and playback actions.
- Player: transport, scrubber with scrub thumbnails, chapter and
  intro/outro skipping, audio and subtitle selection, Next Up, and a stats
  overlay.
- Settings: six pages covering account, playback, audio and subtitles,
  appearance, privacy, and this device. Kodi's own settings dialog is no
  longer used.
- Playback negotiates DirectPlay/DirectFile, so Dolby Vision, TrueHD and
  DTS-HD passthrough and refresh-rate matching reach Kodi's VideoPlayer
  untouched.
- Interface fonts and icons are bundled and installed into the active skin
  at runtime, so the add-on looks the same on any skin.
